\begin{abstract}
This document explains every definition and proved statement in the Lean development, including its regression examples, in mathematical English. Each declaration links to its complete source fragment at a fixed Git revision. The abstract counting-simulation soundness theorem is proved outright; the forward and converse robustness arguments remain conditional on an explicit specimen interface.

The concrete development supplies finite typed graphs, well-formed substitution, operation-path uniqueness, finite boxed hierarchies, exact hole inventories and freshness criteria, recursive isomorphism, transported sites, and nested substitution laws. Globally focus-free and focussed hypernets, actual states, focus insertion and erasure, both round trips, and recursive renaming invariance are formalized. The five coordinate search rules induce deterministic search on recursive state classes. Projection from literal-label classes preserves and lifts executions with exact lengths and reflects search termination. Concrete rootedness and refocusing are defined; search preserves rootedness. Rooted focus sources have operation paths from the unique input, proving the concrete Lemma~23 and the non-operation-path obstruction of Lemma~26(1).

One-way hypernets have their term-to-term path definition, renaming invariance, and filling closure. Path extraction proves the single-hole obstruction of Lemma~26(2), including self-looping holes. Simultaneous shallow substitution now has proved incidence, finiteness, and typing invariants. A concrete pre-template and shallow-specimen representation supplies actual filled states. Output-closure yields the multi-hole obstruction of Lemma~26(3) for these shallow specimens, allowing the one-way side to vary at different holes. Their initial/final agreement also connects the boundary conclusion of Lemma~39(1) directly to concrete specimen states.

Every finite simultaneous shallow expansion is now realized by ordinary addressed substitutions, with a proved finite length bound and order independence up to graph isomorphism. The dependency map distinguishes these checked results from the remaining obligations. Deep specimen substitution, arbitrary independent deep-site interchange, contextual-rewrite adequacy, the full UAM, safety and robustness, and the complete specimen-interface interpretation remain open. This is therefore not yet a formalization of the paper's full Theorem~6.
\end{abstract}
