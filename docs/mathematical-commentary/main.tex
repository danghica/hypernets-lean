\begin{abstract}
This document explains every definition and proved statement in the Lean development, including its regression examples, in mathematical English. Each declaration links to its complete source fragment at a fixed Git revision. The abstract counting-simulation soundness theorem is proved outright; the forward and converse robustness arguments are proved abstractly, and their specimen locality interface now has concrete named-state and state-class instances. Literal class determinism and both concrete counting simulations are proved; concrete observations, initial lifting, and both contextual clauses are now proved for the fixed-depth machine. The unbounded full-machine theorem is also proved in the concrete encoding. Section~\ref{sec:paper-correspondence} explains its correspondence with the paper's Theorem~8.11 and the remaining context-closure and substitution-stability audit questions.

The concrete development supplies finite typed graphs, well-formed substitution, operation-path uniqueness, finite boxed hierarchies, exact hole inventories and freshness criteria, recursive isomorphism, transported sites, and nested substitution laws. Globally focus-free and focussed hypernets, actual states, focus insertion and erasure, both round trips, and recursive renaming invariance are formalized. The five coordinate search rules induce deterministic search on recursive state classes. Projection from literal-label classes preserves and lifts executions with exact lengths and reflects search termination. Concrete rootedness and refocusing are defined; search preserves rootedness. Rooted focus sources have operation paths from the unique input, proving the concrete Lemma~23 and the non-operation-path obstruction of Lemma~26(1).

One-way hypernets have their term-to-term path definition, renaming invariance, and filling closure. Path extraction proves the single-hole obstruction of Lemma~26(2), including self-looping holes. Simultaneous shallow substitution now has proved incidence, finiteness, and typing invariants. A concrete pre-template and shallow-specimen representation supplies actual filled states. Output-closure yields the multi-hole obstruction of Lemma~26(3) for these shallow specimens, allowing the one-way side to vary at different holes. The recursive deep-specimen extension now derives global focus freedom through filled box bodies and proves the same Lemma~26(3), boundary, and jointly rooted context-path conclusions for actual deep-filled states.

Context search transports through recursive fillings with exact lengths and
reflects away from shallow hole boundaries. Erasure now commutes with expansion,
identifying filled-coordinate realizations with the original specimen states.
Concrete entering/exiting classification and the non-exiting conclusion of
Lemma~39(2) are proved. Settled rooted specimens admit synchronized actual search
steps to rooted endpoints at a remote coordinate. Repackaging this successor as
an allowed specimen and proving its full-UAM context-step law are now complete.

Independent original-graph adjacency and incidence equations now characterize
erasure-based state search in both directions. The equivalence also identifies
recursive endpoint renaming. Finite two-edge interaction fragments now factor
through explicit focus-free simple hole contexts in both directions, proving
search adequacy. The proof covers arbitrary simple contexts and self-looping
attachments.

Concrete substitution now applies the explicit Fig.~9(f) fragments in one
simple context. Admissible compute behaviour completes the full UAM transition
relation, with proved mode discipline, disjointness, and final normality.
Actual executions test variable removal and compute behaviour.

Every finite simultaneous shallow expansion is now realized by ordinary addressed substitutions, with a proved finite length bound and order independence up to graph isomorphism. Independent substitutions now commute at arbitrary depth with constructed residual addresses. Hosts with selected sites are quotiented by structural renaming, and substitution and exact freshness descend to these classes. The dependency map distinguishes these checked results from the remaining obligations. Recursive specimens now have complete finite original-hole tuples, with distinct names and closed related replacements. Concrete quasi-specimens, input-safety, and robustness retain the full paper clauses. Full-UAM determinism and refocusing are now reduced to the corresponding behaviour properties, with substitution rootedness and uniqueness proved. The named-state specimen interface and its safety and robustness bridges are now constructed. The full class machine is literally deterministic under the behaviour hypotheses and has concrete forward and converse counting simulations. Concrete initial observation lifting and both contextual clauses are now proved. The operational transfer to the union of all finite depths is proved. This establishes the formal result, subject to the paper-correspondence qualifications in Section~\ref{sec:paper-correspondence}.
Outer set-presentation normalization and depth coherence are proved. A checked
freshening translation now resolves the box-label correspondence: its labels are
actual graphs, it satisfies the paper's equal-label/equal-signature condition,
and encoding and decoding are inverse on recursive graph classes. The earlier
counterexample to naive binder erasure is repaired without restricting the
original typed graphs.
The copying/store extension for Proposition~12.2 now has scoped syntax,
capture-avoiding substitution algebra, a separate typed graph hierarchy,
canonical contraction trees, recursive renaming, and nested replacement laws.
The full Figure~16 translation and intrinsic copying search laws are now checked.
Reference allocation now has a concrete local rule, fresh-store proofs, and
a checked execution of the unchanged reference translation. Dereferencing now has
a general local rule with preserved store identity and an exact execution to
copy request for arbitrary atom trees. The literal copy rule now preserves
residual trees, nested boxes and shared stores, with proved representation laws.
Stable assignment now preserves store identity and old contents with checked
local execution, safety and representation laws. The remaining
compute rules, full-machine laws and the template argument remain pending;
Section~\ref{sec:copying-foundations} records the dependency map and proved
prerequisites.
\end{abstract}
